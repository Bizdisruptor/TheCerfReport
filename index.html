Most commentary tells you what's inevitable.

The Cerf Report treats the world as a series of choices — made by specific people, for specific reasons, and always open to being made differently.

I've spent more than thirty years bringing products and companies to market across the U.S., Europe, and Asia. The first question I learned to ask wasn't "can we do it?" — it was "should we do it?" The Cerf Report applies that same discipline to the systems running the world: economic policy, geopolitical strategy, technology infrastructure, and constitutional governance.

Not "here's what's happening to you." Here's what is actually being chosen, by whom, for what reasons, and what the real alternatives are.

There are always choices.

— David Cerf, Founder & Editor
